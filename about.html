About ENCON Group

Enabling industries to transition towards a low-carbon, energy-efficient, and sustainable future.

Company Profile

Encon Sustainability Pvt Ltd.

Encon Sustainability Pvt. Ltd. (ESPL) is a forward-looking organization committed to enabling industries to transition towards a low-carbon, energy-efficient, and sustainable future. With a strong foundation built on years of experience in energy auditing, regulatory compliance, and sustainability consulting, ESPL serves as a trusted partner for industries navigating the evolving landscape of carbon management and climate responsibility.

Our Background

Promoted by the leadership behind Encon Education and Research Foundation (EERF), ESPL carries forward a legacy of technical excellence, integrity, and impactful implementation. The organization has been actively engaged with national and international frameworks, supporting industries in achieving compliance with energy efficiency norms, carbon markets, and sustainability standards.

Encon Education and Research Foundation (Separate Legal Entity)

Important Notice: Encon Education and Research Foundation (EERF) is a separate legal entity registered under the Societies Registration Act 1860 and the Bombay Public Trust Act 1950. EERF is not a party to any validation or verification engagement undertaken by Encon Sustainability Private Limited.

EERF's main objective has been to contribute towards energy conservation and audit. Net income generated through consultancy and donation would utilize towards the following broad areas: Medical Relief, Education, Energy, Women & Child Health and service, Agriculture, and Social.

EERF is a BEE Empanelment Accredited Energy Auditing Firm (EmAEA) and recognized by BEE and empanelled in energy audit in India. EERF has conducted hundreds of energy audits across sectors including Textile, Sugar, Commercial Buildings, Gold and Diamond Mines, and MSME.
