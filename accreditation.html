Accreditation and Scope

Our authorization and recognized scope of work as an Accredited Carbon Verification Agency.

Accreditation Details

Encon Sustainability Private Limited is listed by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency as an Accredited Carbon Verification Agency under the Carbon Credit Trading Scheme.

Key Information

  • Accreditation Number: ACVA007
  • Status: Provisional accreditation
  • Mechanism: Compliance mechanism
  • Sectors: Iron and Steel; Textile
  • Valid Until: [date as recorded by BEE]

Important Note

The authoritative record of our accreditation is the list of Accredited Carbon Verification Agencies published by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency. Where this page and that list differ, the BEE list prevails.

Scope of Services

We undertake validation and verification services only within the mechanism and sectors stated above. Where an enquiry falls outside our scope we say so, and where we can, name the register in which an appropriately accredited body may be found.

NABCB Accreditation Status

Encon Sustainability Private Limited is not at present accredited by the National Accreditation Board for Certification Bodies. An application for accreditation against ISO/IEC 17029:2019 and ISO 14065:2020 is in preparation. Until accreditation is granted we make no claim of NABCB accreditation.

Enquiry Screening

Every enquiry is screened against these scope boundaries before we accept it, and the screening is recorded. If our scope boundaries mean we must decline your work, we will tell you plainly and promptly.
