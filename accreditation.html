Accreditation and Scope

Our authorization and recognized scope of work as an Accredited Carbon Verification Agency.

Accreditation Details

Encon Sustainability Private Limited is listed by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) as an Accredited Carbon Verification Agency under the Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS): Accreditation Number ACVA007; Status: Provisional accreditation; Mechanism: Compliance mechanism; Sectors: Iron and Steel; Textile; Validity: 02-06-2027.

Important Note

The authoritative record of our accreditation is the list of Accredited Carbon Verification Agencies published by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency. Where this page and that list differ, the BEE list prevails.

Scope of Services

We provide verification services only within the mechanism and sectors stated above. Where an enquiry falls outside our scope we say so, and where we can, name the register in which an appropriately accredited body may be found.

NABCB Accreditation Status

Encon Sustainability Private Limited is not accredited by the National Accreditation Board for Certification Bodies and makes no claim of NABCB accreditation.

Enquiry Screening

Every enquiry is screened against these scope boundaries before we accept it, and the screening is recorded. If our scope boundaries mean we must decline your work, we will tell you plainly and promptly.

About Encon Sustainability Private Limited

Encon Sustainability Private Limited provides independent verification of greenhouse-gas emissions reports within its published scope.
