What Is ADEETIE Energy Efficiency Assessment?

ADEETIE (Assistance in Deploying Energy Efficient Technologies in Industries and Establishments) is a scheme implemented by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), Ministry of Power, Government of India, to help MSMEs adopt energy-efficient technologies and reduce energy consumption.

The scheme supports Investment Grade Energy Audits (IGEA), Detailed Project Reports (DPRs), implementation of energy-saving measures and post-implementation Monitoring and Verification (M&V).

3. How Does the Assessment Work?

Our Assessment Process

The assessment follows a structured process from facility review through post-implementation performance evaluation.

  1. 1Initial Assessment
    • Understand the facility, production processes and energy consumption.
  2. 2Data Collection
    • Review electricity bills, fuel records, production data and equipment details.
  3. 3Energy Audit
    • Identify inefficiencies and opportunities for energy conservation.
  1. 4Technology Evaluation
    • Assess suitable energy-efficient technologies and measures.
  2. 5IGEA & DPR Preparation
    • Document savings, investment costs and project feasibility.
  3. 6Implementation & M&V
    • Evaluate performance and energy savings achieved after implementation.
