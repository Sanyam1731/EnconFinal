Complaints and Appeals

A direct route to raise concerns about our service or challenge a validation or verification decision.

How to submit

Email complaints@enconerf.com. This route is open to clients and other interested parties. You may submit a complaint about our conduct or service, or an appeal asking us to review a validation or verification decision.

Please include your name and contact details, the relevant project or report reference (if available), what happened or which decision you are challenging, the reasons for your concern, and any supporting information. Please say what outcome you are seeking. If you need an accessible alternative to email, contact us at the same address and state your preferred communication method.

Review and response

  1. We acknowledge receipt and may contact you if clarification or further information is needed.
  2. We determine whether the matter is a complaint, an appeal, or both, and explain the review route.
  3. The matter is reviewed objectively by personnel not responsible for the issue or original decision, where practicable. If a conflict cannot be avoided, we explain the alternative arrangements.
  4. We review relevant records and evidence, may seek responses from people involved, and keep the parties informed about progress.
  5. We provide a written outcome with the reasons and any action to be taken. The outcome does not depend on whether the finding is favourable to ENCON or the client.

Confidentiality and impartiality

Information is handled confidentially and shared only with those who need it to review the matter, unless disclosure is required by law or applicable requirements. Raising a concern will not affect the impartiality of an ongoing or future assessment.

If your concern relates to a matter outside our authorized scope or you are unsure whether it qualifies as an appeal, email us and we will clarify the appropriate route.
