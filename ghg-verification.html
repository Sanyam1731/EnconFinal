Greenhouse-Gas Emissions Report Verification

We verify greenhouse-gas emissions reports for obligated entities under the Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS) compliance mechanism, within the sectors and scope covered by our current BEE authorization.

Every enquiry is screened for scope, competence, and impartiality before acceptance. We do not provide advisory, consulting, project-design, emissions-calculation, or report-preparation services for a claim that we may verify.

The current BEE register and the written engagement determine the applicable scope and criteria. For scope questions, contact vvb@enconerf.com.

What to expect

Our Verification Process

Follow the seven stages from initial enquiry through independent review, the verification decision, and after-issue record keeping.

  1. 1Enquiry and pre-engagement
    • We check scope, competence, impartiality, and clarity of criteria before offering terms.
    • Every enquiry is recorded, including declines.
  2. 2Engagement
    • A written contract covers scope, criteria, period, boundary, assurance level, fees, confidentiality, and observation rights.
  3. 3Planning
    • Risk and materiality assessment.
    • Sampling approach.
    • Team appointment (team leader, verifiers, technical experts).
  4. 4Execution
    • Objective evidence is gathered and traced to its source.
    • Findings are raised in writing during the work so you can respond.
  1. 5Independent review
    • The whole engagement is reviewed before any opinion is issued, by someone who was not on the team.
  2. 6Decision and issue of the opinion
    • Taken by an authorised person who did not do the work.
    • Issued as unmodified, modified, adverse, or disclaimed, with reasons.
  3. 7After issue
    • We maintain complete records and follow our procedures for re-issue or withdrawal.
