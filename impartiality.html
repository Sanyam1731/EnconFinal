Impartiality Statement

Our commitment to objective, independent verification.

Commitment

Encon Sustainability Private Limited is committed to impartiality in all verification activities. Conclusions are based on objective evidence and applicable criteria. We do not allow commercial, financial, or other pressures to influence assessment work or its outcome.

Identifying and addressing risks

Before accepting an engagement, and when relevant circumstances change, we consider threats to impartiality. These may arise from self-review, self-interest, advocacy, familiarity, intimidation, personal or business relationships, or other conflicts involving the organization or assigned personnel.

We document identified risks and the controls used to address them. Controls may include changing assigned personnel, independent review, limiting the engagement, or declining the work. Where a threat cannot be effectively managed, we do not accept or continue the engagement.

Independence of assessment

  • Personnel do not assess work for which they were responsible for preparing the claim or its supporting evidence.
  • Assessment conclusions are made by authorized personnel using documented evidence and criteria.
  • Personnel must disclose relevant interests and relationships and cooperate with impartiality reviews.
  • Clients cannot purchase a favourable conclusion; fees are not contingent on the result.

Raise a concern

Anyone who believes impartiality may have been compromised can raise a concern confidentially at complaints@enconerf.com. Concerns are reviewed by personnel not involved in the matter where practicable. A concern may also be submitted through the complaints and appeals process.
