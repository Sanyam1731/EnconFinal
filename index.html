Separate legal entity

Encon Education and Research Foundation (EERF)

Encon Education and Research Foundation (EERF) is a separate legal entity registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 and the Bombay Public Trust Act, 1950. EERF is not a party to verification engagements undertaken by Encon Sustainability Private Limited.

How we work

Independent Verification Principles

Competence

Personnel are assigned according to the engagement's scope and required competence.

Evidence

Assessments use documented evidence relevant to the applicable criteria.

Impartiality

Potential conflicts are assessed before work is accepted.

Independent review

Findings are reviewed before an authorized conclusion is issued.

Confidentiality

Engagement information is handled under applicable confidentiality arrangements.

Defined scope

We decline requests outside our authorization or competence.
