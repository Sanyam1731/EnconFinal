2. NISST

Green Steel Verification Services

Independent Monitoring, Reporting and Verification of greenhouse-gas emissions associated with steel production.

What Is Green Steel Monitoring & Verification?

Green Steel Monitoring, Reporting and Verification (MRV) is the process of assessing and independently verifying greenhouse-gas emissions associated with steel production. It ensures that emissions data and calculations are reliable and comply with applicable NISST Green Steel Certification guidelines.

Key Focus Areas: Emissions Monitoring | Data Accuracy | Independent Verification | Green Steel Certification

2. How Does the Assessment Work?

Our Green Steel Assessment Process

ENCON follows a structured approach to assess emissions data and support Green Steel Certification.

  1. 1Initial Review
    • Understand the plant and production processes.
  2. 2Data Collection
    • Review fuel, electricity, raw materials and production records.
  3. 3On-Site Assessment
    • Examine plant operations and supporting documents.
  1. 4Emissions Verification
    • Assess emissions calculations and emissions intensity.
  2. 5MRV Reporting
    • Prepare and submit the verification report to NISST.
  3. 6Certification
    • NISST reviews the report and determines certification and star rating eligibility.

3. What Does Green Steel Verification Involve?

Verification Scope

Green Steel verification assesses emissions data, calculation methods and supporting records to determine the greenhouse-gas emissions intensity of finished steel.

01

Scope 1

Direct emissions from production processes and fuel consumption.

02

Scope 2

Indirect emissions from purchased electricity.

03

Scope 3

Applicable upstream emissions within the prescribed boundary.

04

Production Data

Verification of production quantities and records.

05

Emissions Intensity

Assessment of greenhouse-gas emissions per tonne of finished steel.

06

MRV Report

Documentation of verification findings for submission to NISST.
