Confidentiality and Use of Opinions and Marks

Public rules for references to verification work and protected information.

Confidentiality

Information received from a client for an engagement is handled as confidential, subject to disclosures required by law, the applicable programme, or the engagement terms. Access is limited to people who need the information for the work or required independent review. Any required disclosure will be handled in accordance with the applicable requirements and engagement terms.

Reference to an opinion

  • Reproduce a verification opinion or report only in its entirety and with its qualifications and status intact; do not present an extract or paraphrase as the opinion.
  • Identify the opinion by its reference, date, entity, statement, reporting period, and criteria where those details are available.
  • A verification opinion addresses the claim assessed. Do not present it as product, system, organization, or person certification.
  • Do not use the company's name, logo, or BEE mark to imply endorsement or accreditation beyond the official register and written authorization.
  • Do not use an opinion that has been withdrawn, amended, or superseded as current.

Contact vvb@enconerf.com before publishing a reference to our work. Concerns about misuse may be raised through our complaints and appeals process.
