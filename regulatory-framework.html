Carbon Credit Trading Scheme

The Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) administers the Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS). Encon Sustainability Private Limited's current public accreditation listing is for the CCTS compliance mechanism and the Iron and Steel and Textile sectors.

The current scheme instruments, applicable detailed procedures, BEE directions, and the written engagement define the criteria for each verification. The official record prevails over summaries on this website.

Official reference

Consult the Bureau of Energy Efficiency for current scheme materials and the authoritative list of Accredited Carbon Verification Agencies. Confirm the applicable version and requirements directly with BEE before relying on a specific document.

Encon Sustainability Private Limited does not claim NABCB accreditation. We will update this page if our authorization or the applicable official framework changes.
