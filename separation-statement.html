What We Do, and What We Deliberately Do Not Do

Our role, scope, and independence boundaries to maintain integrity and objectivity.

Our Role and Boundaries

What We Are: Verification Body

Encon Sustainability Private Limited is an independent verification body. It verifies greenhouse-gas emissions reports only within its current authorization, competence, and published scope.

What We Are Not: Advisory and Consultancy

Encon Sustainability Private Limited does not provide consultancy. We do not:

  • Develop monitoring plans
  • Prepare or review greenhouse gas reports
  • Design compliance or target strategies
  • Advise on the generation or trading of carbon credits
  • Act in any capacity that would put us in the position of assessing our own work

Separation of Services

Encon Sustainability Private Limited does not provide advisory, audit, or training services in the course of its verification work. The verification engagement must remain independent of any consultancy or preparation work relating to the claim under assessment.

Important Restriction

Where an organisation has received advisory or preparation services relating to a claim, Encon Sustainability Private Limited does not verify that claim.

Enquiry Screening

Every enquiry is screened against this rule before we accept it, and the screening is recorded. If the rule means we must decline your work, we will tell you plainly and promptly.

Why This Matters

This clear separation supports independence and objectivity in verification under the Carbon Credit Trading Scheme.

About Encon Sustainability Private Limited

Encon Sustainability Private Limited provides independent verification of greenhouse-gas emissions reports within its published scope.
