What We Do, and What We Deliberately Do Not Do

Our role, scope, and independence boundaries to maintain integrity and objectivity.

Our Role and Boundaries

What We Are: Validation and Verification Body

Encon Sustainability Private Limited is a validation and verification body. Its only business is the independent assessment of environmental information — greenhouse gas reports, claims and statements — against declared criteria.

What We Are Not: Advisory and Consultancy

Encon Sustainability Private Limited does not provide consultancy. We do not:

  • Develop monitoring plans
  • Prepare or review greenhouse gas reports
  • Design compliance or target strategies
  • Advise on the generation or trading of carbon credits
  • Act in any capacity that would put us in the position of assessing our own work

Separation of Services

Advisory, energy-audit and training services are provided by [name of the separate entity], a separate legal entity with its own management, its own personnel and its own finances.

Important Restriction

Where an organisation has received services from [name of the separate entity] relating to a claim, Encon Sustainability Private Limited does not validate or verify that claim.

Enquiry Screening

Every enquiry is screened against this rule before we accept it, and the screening is recorded. If the rule means we must decline your work, we will tell you plainly and promptly.

Why This Matters

This clear separation maintains our independence and objectivity. It ensures that when we assess your environmental claims, we are doing so as an impartial third party, not reviewing our own work. This is fundamental to the credibility of verification under the Carbon Credit Trading Scheme and other regulatory frameworks.
