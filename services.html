CCTS Compliance Mechanism

Greenhouse-Gas Emissions Report Verification

Encon Sustainability Private Limited provides verification of greenhouse-gas emissions reports for obligated entities under the Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS) compliance mechanism, within the Iron and Steel and Textile sectors and subject to the current BEE authorization.

Each enquiry is screened for scope, competence, and impartiality before acceptance. We do not provide advisory, consulting, project-design, emissions-calculation, or report-preparation services for a claim that we may verify.

We do not offer offset-mechanism verification, work outside the sectors named above, or product, management-system, or organization certification. The current BEE register and the written engagement define the applicable scope and criteria.

For scope questions, contact vvb@enconerf.com. See our verification process, impartiality statement, and published activities and standards.
