Carbon Credit Advisory & ICM Support

Carbon footprint assessment & baseline development
We assess your organization's current carbon footprint and establish a reliable baseline covering all emission sources. This is the reference point from which reduction targets are set and carbon credit eligibility is determined under India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS).
Identification of emission reduction opportunities
Our team analyses your operations across the value chain to pinpoint where emissions can be cut most effectively. We prioritize opportunities by cost-effectiveness and carbon impact, giving you a clear, actionable roadmap for strategic decarbonization.
Project eligibility for carbon credit generation
We evaluate whether your emission reduction initiatives qualify for carbon credit generation under CCTS and voluntary mechanisms. This includes methodology review, additionality checks, and alignment with national climate goals — so your project is credible and registerable.
End-to-end MRV — Monitoring, Reporting & Verification
We provide complete MRV support — the backbone of any credible carbon credit project. This covers data collection systems, reporting formats, third-party verification coordination, and ongoing compliance tracking throughout the project lifecycle.
Carbon credit trading & portfolio management strategy
We guide organizations on how to effectively trade earned carbon credits in the Indian Carbon Market and voluntary markets. Our strategic advisory maximizes financial returns while managing risk and maintaining regulatory compliance.

GHG Emissions & Carbon Footprinting

Scope 1, 2 & 3 emissions assessment
We assess all three scopes of greenhouse gas emissions: Scope 1 (direct emissions), Scope 2 (purchased electricity), and Scope 3 (value chain). This complete picture follows GHG Protocol and ISO 14064 standards and gives you a defensible carbon account.
Data collection, validation & emission calculations
We establish structured data collection systems, validate all inputs for quality and consistency, and apply industry-approved emission factors to calculate your precise GHG output — ensuring your carbon accounts are accurate and audit-ready at any time.
Emission hotspot identification & decarbonization strategy
We analyse your emissions profile to pinpoint the largest contributors and translate these into concrete decarbonization opportunities. This lets you focus investment where it delivers the greatest carbon reduction impact at the lowest cost.
Regulatory reporting & sustainability disclosures
We prepare GHG reports aligned with BEE, MoEFCC, and global frameworks such as GRI, BRSR, CDP, and TCFD. Accurate disclosures build investor trust and ensure compliance with national and international sustainability reporting expectations.

PAT — Perform, Achieve & Trade

Setting & monitoring SEC targets for Designated Consumers
We work with Designated Consumers to establish Specific Energy Consumption (SEC) targets as mandated by BEE under the PAT scheme. Throughout the cycle we monitor consumption data, track progress, and flag risks of non-compliance early — so there are no surprises at the end of the cycle.
Gate-to-gate SEC calculation & verification coordination
Accurate SEC calculation using the gate-to-gate approach is essential. We manage the complete calculation process and coordinate with accredited agencies for third-party verification — ensuring your data is accurate and formally accepted by BEE.
Earning & trading Energy Saving Certificates (ESCerts)
Organizations that over-achieve SEC targets earn ESCerts which can be traded on power exchanges. We guide you through qualifying for ESCerts, registering them, and strategically trading them for financial benefit — or purchasing them if you fall short of targets.
Penalty risk minimization & energy savings maximization
Non-compliance with PAT targets attracts financial penalties. We provide proactive monitoring and corrective action support throughout the PAT cycle so your organization avoids penalties while maximizing genuine energy savings and associated cost reductions.
🧩

Renewable Consumption Obligation (RCO)

Obligation assessment & gap analysis
We determine your RCO obligations under the Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022 and compare them against your current renewable energy consumption. The gap analysis tells you exactly how much additional renewable energy you need to source to achieve compliance.
RECs procurement & direct renewable sourcing support
We assist in procuring Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs) through power exchanges or arranging direct sourcing via PPAs and open access. We identify the most cost-effective compliance pathway based on your location, consumption pattern, and budget.
BEE data management, reporting & documentation
We handle accurate data management, reporting, and documentation as per BEE guidelines — maintaining records of renewable energy consumed and RECs purchased, and preparing submissions for regulatory review so you are always audit-ready.
🏅

Green Steel Certification Support

GHG emission intensity assessment of steel production
We measure and analyse the greenhouse gas emission intensity of your steel manufacturing processes — covering raw material inputs, production, and energy use. This baseline determines your current green steel rating and charts the path towards certification.
MRV for Scope 1, 2 & 3 emissions
Green steel certification requires robust MRV across all emission scopes. We implement and manage the full MRV framework — from plant-level data collection to third-party verification — ensuring your emission claims are credible and certification-ready.
Green steel rating determination (3-star to 5-star)
India's green steel system awards 3-star to 5-star ratings based on emission intensity benchmarks. We calculate your current rating, identify what operational improvements are needed to achieve a higher rating, and guide you step by step to your target certification level.
Process optimization & decarbonization strategies
Beyond certification, we provide practical decarbonization strategies for your steel plant — including fuel switching, energy efficiency improvements, renewable energy integration, and carbon capture considerations — aligned with improving your green steel rating over time.

Renewable Energy Consulting

Feasibility studies, resource assessment & technology selection
Before committing to a renewable project we conduct detailed feasibility studies — assessing solar, wind, or other resources at your location, analysing grid connectivity, and recommending the most suitable technology. Your investment is based on real data, not assumptions.
Financial analysis — ROI, cost savings & payback period
We build detailed financial models covering capital cost, operational savings, government incentives, and payback period. This empowers you and your stakeholders to make informed investment decisions with confidence in the financial case for going renewable.
Net metering, open access & procurement strategy
We guide you through net metering applications, open access permissions, power purchase agreements (PPAs), and compliance with state and central regulations. Our expertise ensures a smooth and legally compliant transition to renewable energy for your facility.

Monthly Electricity Bill Analysis

Detection of billing errors & incorrect tariff categories
Utility bills frequently contain errors — wrong tariff categories, incorrect demand charges, or missed rebates. We meticulously review your monthly bills to catch these issues, recover overcharges, and ensure you are on the most appropriate tariff slab for your consumption pattern.
Maximum demand, power factor & load pattern evaluation
We analyse your demand curves, power factor readings, and energy consumption trends to identify inefficient loading patterns. Poor power factor and high demand peaks significantly inflate electricity bills — we quantify the problem and recommend targeted corrective actions.
Tariff optimization & demand management recommendations
Based on analysis we recommend practical steps: load shifting to off-peak hours, demand controller installation, power factor correction capacitors, and tariff renegotiation where applicable. Each recommendation is backed by savings calculations so you can prioritize the highest-impact actions first.

Energy Audits — Detailed & Mandatory

Comprehensive analysis of boilers, motors, HVAC & utilities
We conduct in-depth analysis of all major energy-consuming systems — steam boilers, furnaces, electric motors, drives, HVAC equipment, compressed air, lighting, and utilities. Each system is individually assessed for performance against benchmarks and losses are quantified in monetary terms.
Mandatory audit compliance for Designated Consumers
Industries classified as Designated Consumers (DCs) under the Energy Conservation Act are legally required to conduct energy audits. We manage the full process — audit execution, documentation, and submission in BEE and MEDA formats — ensuring you remain compliant and avoid regulatory penalties.
Practical recommendations with cost-benefit & payback analysis
Every audit concludes with a prioritized list of energy-saving measures — each with estimated savings in kWh and ₹, implementation cost, and payback period. Our recommendations are grounded in field measurements and real operational data, making them actionable and bankable.
🧪

Targeted Energy Audits

Deep-dive diagnostics on specific systems (boilers, compressors, HVAC)
When you know a particular system is underperforming, a targeted audit delivers deep-dive diagnostics without the full scope of a plant-wide audit. We bring specialized instruments and expertise to your chosen system and measure actual performance against design and benchmark values.
Quick-win recommendations with cost-benefit analysis
Targeted audits are designed to yield fast, measurable results. We identify both no-cost/low-cost improvements (operational changes, set-point adjustments) and capital investments with short payback periods. Each recommendation includes a clear cost-benefit breakdown so you can act immediately.

Water & Air Audits

Water consumption, leakage detection & recycling opportunities
We map your entire water consumption — intake, process use, cooling, sanitation, and discharge. Using flow measurements and balance studies we identify leakage points, wasteful practices, and opportunities for water recycling and reuse that reduce both water costs and environmental impact.
Compressed air — compressor, pipeline & leakage assessment
Compressed air systems are among the most energy-intensive utilities in manufacturing. We measure compressor efficiency, audit pipeline networks for pressure drops, and use ultrasonic leak detectors to quantify losses. Recommendations typically yield 15–30% energy savings in this system alone.
Water & air balance studies with implementation strategies
We prepare formal water and air balance reports giving a clear picture of resource flows, losses, and inefficiencies. These are accompanied by prioritized implementation strategies with cost and savings estimates for each action — covering both operational improvements and infrastructure upgrades.
🔌

Electrical Safety & Power Quality Audit

Panels, wiring, earthing & protection system inspection
We inspect all major electrical infrastructure — distribution panels, switchgear, cables, earthing systems, and protection relays — to identify safety risks including loose connections, overloaded circuits, inadequate protection, and poor earthing that could lead to equipment failure or fire hazard.
Harmonics, voltage fluctuations & load imbalance analysis
Using power quality analysers we measure voltage levels, harmonic distortion, power factor, load imbalance, and transients at key network points. Poor power quality shortens equipment life and increases energy losses — we quantify the problem and recommend corrective solutions such as harmonic filters and capacitor banks.
❄️

HVAC & Thermal System Optimization

Performance testing of chillers, AHUs, cooling towers & boilers
We conduct detailed performance testing of all HVAC and thermal equipment — chillers, Air Handling Units (AHUs), cooling towers, boilers, and heat exchangers. Each unit is benchmarked against design specifications and industry standards to identify performance gaps, inefficiencies, and maintenance needs.
Heat loss identification, retrofitting & process improvements
Using thermography and flue gas analysis we identify heat losses in insulation, steam traps, and distribution networks. Our recommendations cover retrofitting options (VFDs, efficient burners), process changes (load scheduling, set-point optimization), and automation to lock in energy savings sustainably.

Solar Rooftop Design

System sizing, inverter selection & financial ROI analysis
We design the complete system — panel capacity, inverter type, mounting structure, and protection — optimized for your specific roof and load. Our financial analysis includes capital cost, net metering savings, subsidy benefits, ROI, and payback period so you can make an informed decision.
Net metering approval & regulatory compliance support
We guide you through net metering applications with your DISCOM, assist in obtaining necessary approvals, and ensure the system design meets all relevant standards (CEA, IEC). Our support extends through commissioning so your rooftop system is legally connected and generating from day one.

Energy audit methodologies & measurement techniques
Participants learn the full methodology for conducting energy audits — from pre-audit planning and data collection to on-site measurement techniques and post-audit reporting. Training covers use of instruments (power meters, flue gas analysers, ultrasonic flow meters) and application of BEE audit protocols.
Hands-on analysis of boilers, HVAC, motors & utilities
Beyond theory, participants apply skills through hands-on sessions on real systems. They learn to identify inefficiencies in boilers, HVAC, compressed air, motors, and lighting using live data and practical exercises — building the confidence to conduct audits independently in the field.
BEE standards, regulatory frameworks & reporting formats
Participants gain a thorough understanding of the Energy Conservation Act, BEE guidelines, MEDA requirements, and mandatory audit report formats. This equips them to ensure their audits meet regulatory standards and produce documentation that is accepted by authorities without revision.
Customized on-site or classroom sessions
Programs are tailored to your organization's specific sector, equipment, and skill gaps. We offer both on-site training (using your own equipment) and classroom-based sessions. Duration and depth are adjusted to fit your team's schedule, role, and learning objectives.
