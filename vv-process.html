Validation and Verification Process

How we review enquiries, evidence, and conclusions for in-scope engagements.

Our process

ENCON Sustainability Private Limited undertakes validation and verification only within its current approved scope. Each engagement is conducted against identified criteria and is independent of any consultancy or preparation of the claim being assessed.

1. Enquiry and scope review

We review the requested activity, sector, scheme, applicable criteria, and available information against our current authorization and competence. We explain any scope limits before accepting work. Enquiries outside our approved scope are declined.

2. Impartiality and engagement agreement

Before acceptance, we identify and assess relevant impartiality risks and confirm that suitable competent personnel are available. The written engagement defines the subject, boundaries, criteria, responsibilities, deliverables, and any limitations. Work is not accepted where an impartiality threat cannot be adequately addressed.

3. Planning and evidence gathering

We plan the assessment based on the agreed scope and criteria. The assessment may include document review, data and calculation checks, interviews, traceability checks, and a site visit where appropriate. We record evidence and follow up on gaps or inconsistencies with the responsible client representatives.

4. Independent review and decision

Evidence and findings are reviewed before a conclusion is authorized. The decision is based on the applicable criteria and documented evidence; commercial considerations do not determine the outcome. The client is informed of material findings and given an opportunity to clarify factual points.

5. Reporting and records

We issue the agreed validation or verification report describing the scope, criteria, work performed, conclusion, and relevant limitations or findings. Engagement records are maintained under our document-control and confidentiality arrangements, subject to applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Questions about an engagement may be sent to vvb@enconerf.com. To challenge a decision, use the complaints and appeals process.
