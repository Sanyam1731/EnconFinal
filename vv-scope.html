V&V Activities, Schemes and Standards

Published scope information for ENCON Sustainability Private Limited.

Accreditation and scheme

ENCON Sustainability Private Limited is listed by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) as an Accredited Carbon Verification Agency, accreditation number ACVA007. The published accreditation page records provisional status under the Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS) compliance mechanism, for the Iron and Steel and Textile sectors.

The current BEE register and the agency's formal approval are authoritative for status, validity, sector coverage, and exact activity scope. Contact vvb@enconerf.com to confirm whether a specific project or activity is within scope before engagement.

Activities

  • Validation of carbon project documentation and claims within the approved CCTS scope.
  • Verification of project monitoring reports and reported greenhouse-gas reductions within the approved CCTS scope.

ENCON accepts work only where the activity, sector, and applicable requirements fall within its current authorization and competence. No voluntary carbon program or other sector is represented here as an approved scope.

Applicable criteria and standards

For each engagement, the applicable criteria are the current CCTS legislation and BEE requirements, the approved project methodology and program documents, and any activity-specific requirements identified in the engagement agreement. The precise criteria are confirmed with the client before work begins.

ENCON Sustainability Private Limited does not claim NABCB accreditation to ISO/IEC 17029 or ISO 14065. These standards are not presented as an accreditation held by the organization. Where a client asks for work requiring an accreditation or scope not held, the work will be declined.

For authoritative accreditation status and scope, consult the Bureau of Energy Efficiency register or contact us to request confirmation.

About ENCON

ENCON Sustainability Private Limited provides independent validation and verification of environmental information within its published scope.
